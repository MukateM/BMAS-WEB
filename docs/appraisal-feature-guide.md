# Appraisal Feature Step-by-Step Guide

This guide explains how to run an appraisal cycle from form creation to final sign-off. It uses one running example throughout so the whole workflow is easy to follow.

## Running Example

BMAS wants to run a mid-year appraisal for **Mary Banda**, an **HR Officer**, for the review period **1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026**.

The appraisal will be completed by:

- **Employee:** Mary Banda
- **Line manager:** James Phiri, HR Manager
- **HR reviewer:** Chipo Mwansa, HR Administrator
- **Final approver:** Managing Director

The appraisal form will measure:

- Job objectives
- Key performance indicators
- Behavioural competencies
- Development needs
- Overall rating
- Employee comments
- Manager comments
- Final sign-off

## Step 1: Create the New Appraisal

Start by creating the appraisal record. At this stage, you are setting up the appraisal cycle details, not adding the individual appraisal sections yet.

1. Open the appraisal feature from the HR system menu.
2. Select **New Appraisal**.
3. Enter a clear appraisal name, for example:

   `Mid-Year Performance Appraisal 2026`

4. Add the review period:

   `1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026`

5. Add a short description:

   `This appraisal reviews employee performance, objectives, conduct, development needs, and readiness for the next review period.`

6. Save or continue to move to the employee list.

For the running example, this creates the mid-year appraisal cycle before Mary Banda is selected.

## Step 2: Select Employees from the Employee List

After the appraisal details are saved, the system takes you to the employee list. This is where you choose who the appraisal applies to.

1. Review the employee list.
2. Search or filter by employee name, department, branch, or job title if needed.
3. Select the employee or employees who must complete the appraisal.
4. Confirm the employee selection.
5. Assign or confirm each employee's line manager, HR reviewer, and final approver if the system asks for these details at this stage.

For the running example, select:

- Employee: `Mary Banda`
- Department: `Human Resources`
- Line manager: `James Phiri`
- HR reviewer: `Chipo Mwansa`
- Final approver: `Managing Director`

## Step 3: Add Appraisal Sections

After the employee selection is confirmed, add or configure the appraisal sections for the appraisal. This is the point where the form content is built.

1. Add a **Job Objectives** section.
2. Add a **KPI Review** section.
3. Add a **Behavioural Competencies** section.
4. Add a **Development Plan** section.
5. Add an **Overall Rating** section.
6. Add an **Employee Comments** section.
7. Add a **Manager Comments** section.
8. Add a **Sign-Off** section.

For Mary Banda, example objectives may include:

- Maintain accurate employee files.
- Support recruitment administration.
- Prepare monthly HR reports on time.
- Track leave records and attendance data.
- Support compliance with HR policies.

## Step 4: Configure Rating Scales

Set the scoring method before the form is issued.

1. Open the rating scale settings for the appraisal form.
2. Choose the rating style, such as a 1 to 5 scale.
3. Define what each score means.

Example rating scale:

| Score | Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | Performance is below expected standard and requires urgent improvement. |
| 2 | Needs Improvement | Some requirements are met, but gaps remain. |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Performance meets the role requirements. |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Performance is consistently above expected standard. |
| 5 | Outstanding | Performance is exceptional and creates clear value beyond the role. |

For Mary Banda, the manager will use this scale to rate each objective, KPI, and competency.

## Step 5: Publish or Activate the Appraisal

Once the form is ready, make it available to the selected employees.

1. Review all sections and fields.
2. Confirm that the correct employees, managers, and reviewers are assigned.
3. Set the submission deadline.
4. Publish, activate, or launch the appraisal form.

For the running example:

- Employee submission deadline: `15 July 2026`
- Manager review deadline: `22 July 2026`
- HR review deadline: `25 July 2026`
- Final sign-off deadline: `31 July 2026`

## Step 6: Employee Completes Self-Assessment

The employee starts the appraisal by completing their self-assessment.

1. Mary opens her assigned appraisal.
2. She reviews each objective and KPI.
3. She enters evidence for work completed during the review period.
4. She rates herself where self-rating is enabled.
5. She adds development needs and career comments.
6. She submits the appraisal to her line manager.

Example employee comment:

`I maintained updated HR files, supported three recruitment assignments, and submitted monthly HR reports by the required deadlines. I would like further training in HR analytics and labour law updates.`

## Step 7: Manager Reviews the Appraisal

The line manager reviews the employee submission and adds their assessment.

1. James opens Mary Banda's submitted appraisal.
2. He reviews Mary's self-assessment and supporting comments.
3. He enters manager ratings for each section.
4. He adds comments explaining each rating.
5. He identifies strengths, performance gaps, and development actions.
6. He submits the manager review.

Example manager comment:

`Mary has met expectations during the review period. She has been reliable with HR records and reporting, and she has shown improvement in recruitment coordination. Further development in HR analytics would strengthen her reporting contribution.`

Example manager rating:

`Overall rating: 3 - Meets Expectations`

## Step 8: Hold the Appraisal Discussion

The appraisal should include a discussion between the employee and manager.

1. Schedule a meeting with Mary and James.
2. Review the self-assessment and manager assessment together.
3. Discuss areas of agreement and difference.
4. Confirm development needs.
5. Agree on goals for the next review period.
6. Update the appraisal record if any agreed changes are needed.

For Mary Banda, the agreed development actions are:

- Attend HR analytics training by September 2026.
- Receive monthly coaching on management reporting.
- Lead the preparation of one HR dashboard by the next appraisal cycle.

## Step 9: HR Reviews the Appraisal

HR checks that the appraisal is complete, fair, and properly documented.

1. Chipo opens the submitted appraisal.
2. She confirms that required fields are complete.
3. She checks that ratings have comments or evidence.
4. She verifies that the development plan is practical.
5. She returns the appraisal for correction if anything is missing.
6. If complete, she marks the appraisal as HR reviewed.

For Mary Banda, HR confirms that the appraisal includes employee comments, manager comments, ratings, and agreed development actions.

## Step 10: Final Approval

The final approver confirms that the appraisal can be closed.

1. The Managing Director opens the HR-reviewed appraisal.
2. They review the final rating and development plan.
3. They confirm that HR and the manager have completed their steps.
4. They approve or reject the appraisal.

For Mary Banda, the Managing Director approves the appraisal with the final rating:

`3 - Meets Expectations`

## Step 11: Employee and Manager Sign Off

The appraisal is completed when the required parties sign off.

1. Mary reviews the final appraisal record.
2. Mary adds any final employee acknowledgement comment.
3. Mary signs off.
4. James signs off as line manager.
5. HR confirms the sign-off status.

Example employee acknowledgement:

`I acknowledge that the appraisal discussion was held and that the development actions have been agreed.`

The appraisal status should now show as:

`Completed` or `Signed Off`

## Step 12: Close and Archive the Appraisal

After sign-off, close the appraisal cycle.

1. Confirm that all assigned appraisals have been submitted.
2. Follow up on any overdue appraisals.
3. Export or save appraisal records if required.
4. Archive the completed appraisal cycle.
5. Use the ratings and development plans for HR reporting, training planning, and future performance reviews.

For Mary Banda, the completed record should show:

- Review period: `1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026`
- Final rating: `3 - Meets Expectations`
- Status: `Signed Off`
- Development actions: HR analytics training, reporting coaching, HR dashboard preparation

## End-to-End Workflow Summary

1. HR clicks **New Appraisal** and enters the appraisal name, review period, and description.
2. The system opens the employee list.
3. HR selects the employees who will be appraised.
4. HR confirms the manager, HR reviewer, and final approver for the selected employees.
5. HR adds appraisal sections, objectives, rating scales, and deadlines.
6. HR publishes the appraisal cycle.
7. The employee completes the self-assessment.
8. The manager completes the performance review.
9. The employee and manager hold the appraisal discussion.
10. HR reviews the completed appraisal.
11. The final approver approves the appraisal.
12. The employee and manager sign off.
13. HR closes and archives the appraisal.
